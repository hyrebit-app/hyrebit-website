HyreBit Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy details how HyreBit collects, safeguards, and respects your personal and location data when connecting local businesses and job seekers.

Effective Date: June 20, 2026 • Version: 2.4 (Latest) • Jurisdiction: Global & Local Labor Standards

Our Core Privacy Principles (In Plain English)

Hyperlocal by Design

We only use your GPS location to display relevant jobs within your selected neighborhood radius (e.g., 5km). We never track you when the app is closed unless explicitly enabled for shifts.

We Never Sell Your Data

HyreBit does not monetize personal data through third-party data brokers. Your profile is only shared with verified local business managers when you choose to apply.

Full Account & Data Control

You retain complete rights to access, download, or permanently delete your account and personal records at any time directly through in-app settings or email request.

Who We Are & Scope of this Policy

Welcome to HyreBit ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by HyreBit Technologies Inc. This Privacy Policy governs your use of our mobile applications, web platform located at https://hyrebit.com, and associated local staffing and wage fulfillment services (collectively, the "Platform").

HyreBit bridges the gap between neighborhood job seekers ("Earners" or "Job Seekers") and local storefronts, retail outlets, supermarkets, and cafes ("Employers" or "Partners"). Because our core mission involves matchmaking within localized radiuses, our platform relies on specific location and identity information. This document explains transparently how that information is handled.

Important: By downloading our mobile app, registering an account, posting an opening, or tapping "Quick Apply," you agree to the collection and handling of your data as outlined in this document.
2

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to verify identity, foster trust, and deliver rapid job matchmaking:

A. For Job Seekers & Gig Earners

  • Account Basics: Full name, verified mobile phone number, and optional email address.
  • Profile & Qualifications: Skills (e.g., Barista, Cashier, Inventory), spoken languages, availability preferences, and prior work experience.
  • Application History: Gigs applied for, accepted shifts, clock-in timestamps, and shop ratings.
  • Payout Information: Bank account, UPI ID, or digital wallet details necessary to process direct wage payouts.

B. For Local Employers & Business Owners

  • Business Profile: Registered store/cafe name, manager contact name, official phone number, business email, and storefront address.
  • Verification Credentials: Business permits, GST numbers, or municipal trade licenses to qualify for the "Verified Partner" badge.
  • Hiring Listings: Shift hours, job requirements, wage rates, and manager review remarks.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Information

  • Device model, Operating System version (iOS/Android), unique device identifiers, IP address, crash analytics, and platform interaction logs.
3

Location & GPS Data Usage

HyreBit’s core value proposition is instant local hiring. To display opportunities located within walking or quick commuting distance (such as within 1km to 5km), our application requests access to your device's location services.

Foreground Location

Accessed while actively browsing the app to calculate proximity tags (e.g. "0.8 km away") and prioritize nearby job listings.

Geofence Shift Verification

Used during confirmed work shifts to verify shift arrival and enable instantaneous wage release upon completion.

Your Control: You may grant or withdraw location permissions anytime through your device’s operating system settings (Settings > HyreBit > Location Permissions). Note that disabling location may limit job discovery to manual neighborhood search.

Information Sharing & Disclosure

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertising companies. We only share information in the following clearly defined circumstances:

  • Between Job Seekers & Verified Employers: When an earner submits a job application, their contact name, phone, listed skills, and distance are transmitted to the verified employer to review the hiring request.
  • Service Providers & Infrastructure: Trusted cloud hosting providers (e.g., AWS/Google Cloud), SMS verification delivery gateways, and regulated payment processors bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Mandates: When legally compelled by court orders, government authorities, or to protect the vital safety of individuals on our platform.
6

Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide services and comply with financial record-keeping standards (such as payroll audit logs).

How to Permanently Delete Your Data

You can permanently delete your profile at any time by going to HyreBit App > Settings > Privacy & Security > Delete Account, or by emailing privacy@hyrebit.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

Upon confirmation, all your profile data, personal identifiers, and application records are scrubbed from active databases within 14 business days, except records legally required for tax compliance.

7

Security Safeguards

HyreBit implements defense-in-depth technical and organizational measures to safeguard user data:

256-Bit TLS Encryption

All data transmitted between mobile devices and servers is encrypted in transit.

Role-Based Access

Strict internal least-privilege policies protect stored databases and payment tokens.

Vetted Partners

Employer stores undergo verification before they can post shifts and receive applicants.

8

Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction (such as GDPR, CCPA, or regional personal data protection regulations), you possess specific legal rights regarding your information:

  • Right to Access: Request a full copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Update outdated contact details or resume info instantly.
  • Data Portability: Export your shift history and profile in a structured format.
  • Opt-out of Notifications: Manage SMS or push notification preferences in settings.
9

Cookies & Web Technologies

Our landing website (hyrebit.com) uses minimal functional cookies and session tokens to preserve authentication states and analyze anonymized site traffic. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings without impacting core website viewing.

10

Children's Privacy Protection

HyreBit is strictly designed for individuals who have reached the legal working age in their relevant jurisdiction (minimum 18 years of age, or 16+ where legally permitted by local labor frameworks with parental consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we discover an account registered by an ineligible minor, it will be promptly suspended and deleted.

When material updates occur, we will notify you through an in-app banner or SMS notification prior to the change becoming effective. The revised "Last Updated" date at the top will indicate when modifications took effect.
